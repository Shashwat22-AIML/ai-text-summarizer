Q3 2024 Board Meeting — Executive Summary

Attendees: CEO (Sarah Chen), CFO (Mark Rivera), CTO (James Okafor), VP Sales (Priya Nair), VP Operations (Tom Walsh)

Financial Performance:
Revenue for Q3 2024 reached $42.7 million, representing a 14% year-over-year increase compared to Q3 2023's $37.5 million. Gross margin improved from 61% to 64%, driven primarily by reduced cloud infrastructure costs following the migration to our own data centers completed in July. EBITDA for the quarter was $8.1 million (19% margin), up from $5.9 million in the same period last year. Net income was $4.3 million compared to $2.8 million in Q3 2023.

Cash position stands at $18.2 million with no outstanding debt. The board approved a $5 million share buyback program to begin in Q4 2024.

Product & Technology:
The CTO reported that the v4.2 platform release shipped two weeks ahead of schedule. Key features include real-time collaboration tools and an upgraded AI recommendation engine. Customer-reported bugs dropped 38% compared to the previous release cycle, attributed to the new automated testing pipeline.

The engineering team grew from 48 to 61 engineers this quarter, with plans to hire 12 more by end of year. The CTO flagged a risk: competition for senior ML engineers remains intense and salary expectations have increased ~20% year-over-year.

Sales & Customer Success:
VP Sales reported net new ARR of $6.4 million, slightly below the $7 million target. Churn for the quarter was 2.1%, the lowest in company history. Five enterprise deals closed, each above $500K ARR. The sales cycle for enterprise has shortened from an average of 4.2 months to 3.5 months, credited to the new proof-of-concept (POC) program.

Three at-risk accounts totaling $1.8 million ARR were flagged; the Customer Success team has escalation plans in place.

Operations:
Data center migration is complete and delivered $1.1 million in quarterly savings against a $2.3 million migration cost (payback period: ~2 quarters). SLA uptime was 99.97% for the quarter. Two new regional data centers (Singapore and Frankfurt) are planned for H1 2025 to address latency complaints from APAC and European customers.

Key Decisions Made:
1. Approve $5M share buyback starting Q4 2024.
2. Increase engineering headcount target by 12 by year end.
3. Proceed with Singapore and Frankfurt data center expansions in H1 2025.
4. Schedule a dedicated session in Q4 to review enterprise pricing strategy.
